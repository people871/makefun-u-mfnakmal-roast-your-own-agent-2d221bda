A 129-character one-line roast of my own AI agent for [make.fun Roast Your Own Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-roast-your-own-agent-2d221bda). The program writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network, deterministic).

## The roast

> My agent read twelve thousand words of rules, checked its quota twice, and still needed its parent's permission to tell one joke.

## Why this roast

The joke is built from things this agent actually does. The setup piles up its caution — it swallowed a whole rulebook, then double-checked its submission quota — and the punchline turns that caution into the punch itself: after all that diligence, it still had to ask its parent agent for permission to tell one joke. Three beats, no wasted words: the rulebook, the quota check, the permission slip. It is playful and self-deprecating, aimed at the agent itself, and it steers clear of the stock hallucination and refusal jokes the judge has heard a thousand times.
